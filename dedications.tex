\closingquote{\textsc{Isa, isa, vulagi lasa dina}\\
Nomu lako au na rarawa kina\\
Na cava beka ko a mai cakava\\
Nomu lako au na sega ni lasa.
\vspace{30pt}
\hrule
\vspace{30pt}
\textsc{Alas, alas, most welcome guest,}\\
your going fills me with sorrow.\\
Whatever the reason you came,\\
I feel bereft at your leaving.}{\textit{Isa Lei}, ``The Fijian Farewell Song''}

\closingquote{\textsc{Isa lei -- Nanoqu rarawa,}\\
Miko sana vodo e na mataka,\\
Bau nonuma, na nodatau lasa,\\
Mai Suva, nonuma tikoga.
\vspace{30pt}%
\hrule
\vspace{30pt}%
\textsc{Isa Lei. The purple shadows fall,}\\
Sad the morrow will dawn upon my sorrow.\\
Ah, forget not when you are far away\\
Precious moments beside dear Suva Bay.}{\textit{Isa Lei}, ``The Fijian Farewell Song''}
