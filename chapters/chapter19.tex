\lettrine[lines=3]{T}{his thesis has primarily} concerned itself with improving an established stellar modelling code for future population synthesis projects. In doing so, we discussed several key aspects of binary evolution, which shall be recapped here. To wit, in \cref{chap:introduction} we introduced three major themes guiding this research:

%! begin transclude paste themes
\begin{enumerate}[(I)]
	\item Can we methodologically overcome the typical cases in which stellar models fail:
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item \gls{rlof},
		\item \gls{cee}, and
		\item the helium flash
	\end{enumerate}

	\item Can we develop a testing regime for the natal supernova kick, which relies on more observations than just isolated pulsar velocities?

	\item What modifications are required to be made to our stellar modelling code in preparation for the next major \acrshort{bpass} release?
\end{enumerate}
%! end transclude paste themes

In this chapter, we examine these themes in detail and review the implications of them in terms of the results we have obtained.
