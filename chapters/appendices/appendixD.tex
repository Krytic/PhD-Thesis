\chapter{Period-Eccentricity distributions along the \acrshort{lvk} \acrshort{o3} contour}\label{chap:ligo_contour_results}

In \autoref{fig:o3a_contour_figure} we display the period-eccentricity distributions along the \gls{lvk} \gls{o3} contour. We also display a white dashed line corresponding to a merger time contour of the Hubble time. Most plots have a white region in the lower right corner of the plot, which corresponds to no systems passing through those bins. We use the definition of a \gls{mweg} from \citet{kasen_origin_2017}, namely that there is one \gls{mweg} per $(\SI{4.4}{\mega\pc})^3$.
